# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Domestique to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their experience level, background or identity. We pledge to make taking part in this community a harassment-free experience for all.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- Being kind, patient and constructive, especially with newcomers
- Respecting different viewpoints and experiences. Disagreeing about design is healthy and encouraged.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome advances
- Trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews, and when someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through [GitHub](https://github.com/arbarlow). All reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy will be respected.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that don't follow this Code of Conduct. They may also temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they judge inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful.
